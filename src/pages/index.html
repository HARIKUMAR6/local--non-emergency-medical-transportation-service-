How it works

A more comfortable journey

Three simple stages, so passengers and families know what happens next.

  1. Ahead of the appointment

    Arrange the ride

    Request a ride online or by phone with the appointment date, time and address. Let us know about mobility needs, equipment, and whether someone will ride along.

  2. Before the trip

    Confirm pickup details

    We confirm the pickup time and place, the destination entrance, and the plan for the return trip, so everyone knows what to expect.

  3. On the day

    Prepare for pickup and the visit

    Have ID, insurance or payment details, a medication list and any paperwork ready. Your driver helps from the door to the vehicle, as arranged when you booked.
