The ride journey

From first call to front door, in three stages.

Each stage has one clear job, so nothing is left to the last minute.

  1. Stage 1

    Arrange the ride

    Request a ride online or by phone. Share the appointment time and address, the pickup address, and any mobility needs.

    Booking for someone else? You can arrange the ride on their behalf.

  2. Stage 2

    Confirm pickup and destination

    We confirm the pickup time, the exact entrance at the destination, and how the return trip will work.

    Check the details against the appointment letter or reminder.

  3. Stage 3

    Prepare for pickup and travel

    Be ready a little before pickup, with paperwork, medication and any mobility aids that travel with you.

    Your driver assists to the level agreed when you booked.

What you’ll be asked when arranging a ride

  1. The passenger’s name and a contact number
  2. Pickup address, and any access notes such as steps or gate codes
  3. Appointment address, date and time
  4. Mobility needs and equipment that will travel
  5. Whether a family member or carer will ride along
  6. Plans for the return trip

For families and caregivers

Practical tips for a smoother ride.

Whether you’re booking for yourself or someone you care for, these steps help the day go to plan.

Confirm pickup and appointment details

Double-check the appointment time, building and entrance. Let us know how early the clinic asks patients to arrive.

Share mobility and assistance needs

Mention walkers, wheelchairs, steps at home or any equipment that travels with the passenger, so suitable help can be arranged where available.

Plan ahead for recurring visits

Share the full treatment schedule early. A regular pickup time is easier to keep than booking each ride separately.

Tell us when trip details change

If an appointment moves, runs late or is cancelled, contact us as soon as you can so the ride can be updated.
