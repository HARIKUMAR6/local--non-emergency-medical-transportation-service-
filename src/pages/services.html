Overview

We provide scheduled, non-emergency transportation for people who need a little more support getting to and from healthcare: one-off appointments, regular treatment, and other trips arranged around a medical visit.

Ways we can help you travel

Medical and specialist appointments

A ride to check-ups, specialist visits, scans and tests, with the trip home planned at the same time.

Recurring treatment

Regular rides for ongoing care such as therapy or treatment sessions, set up as a schedule rather than booked one by one.

Where offered

Returning home from hospital

A calm ride home after a hospital stay or outpatient procedure, planned around the discharge time you’re given.

Available by arrangement

Wheelchair-accessible transportation

For passengers who travel in their own wheelchair or need a folding chair carried. Accessible vehicles depend on availability in your area, so we confirm what’s possible when you enquire.

Choosing the right ride

Have these four details ready when you get in touch. They tell us which service fits.

  1. Appointment date and time

    And how early the clinic asks you to arrive.

  2. Pickup and destination

    Including the building entrance and any steps.

  3. Mobility needs

    Wheelchair, walker, or help to and from the door.

  4. Return or regular trips

    Whether you need a ride home or a recurring schedule.

Not sure which service fits?

Tell us about the trip and we’ll talk you through the options.
