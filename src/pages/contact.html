What to include

If you’re asking about a ride, these details help us reply with something useful.

Phone and email: direct contact details haven’t been published on this website yet, so please use the form.

  • Who is travelling
    Yourself, or someone you care for
  • Appointment date and time
    And when you need to arrive
  • Pickup and destination
    Town or area is enough to start
  • Mobility needs
    Wheelchair, walker or help at the door
  • Return or regular trips
    A ride home, or a recurring schedule
  • How to reach you
    Your preferred way to be contacted
