About

Getting to care should feel calm, not complicated.

We arrange planned, non-emergency rides for people who need a little more support on the way to their appointments.

Our approach

Transport that fits around the person travelling

What non-emergency medical transportation is
Planned transport to and from healthcare for people who don’t need an ambulance, but may find ordinary travel difficult because of age, mobility, illness or a recent procedure.
How we work
Each trip is planned around the passenger: their pace, the help they need at the door, and the appointment waiting at the other end. Family members and carers are welcome to arrange rides and stay involved.

What matters to us

01

Dignity in every detail

Passengers are spoken to directly, helped at their own pace, and treated with patience and privacy from the front door onwards.

02

Clear, honest communication

We explain what’s included and what’s possible, and say plainly when something isn’t, before a ride is arranged.

03

Planned around the appointment

Pickup times are worked back from when you need to arrive, with time to check in.

04

Families kept involved

Relatives and carers can arrange rides on someone’s behalf and share a number to be reached on the day.

How each journey is coordinated

Three steps between your first message and the day of travel.

  1. Understand ride needs

    We ask about mobility, equipment, companions and any access details at home.

  2. Coordinate trip details

    The pickup time, destination entrance and return plan are agreed with you.

  3. Prepare for the journey

    You know when to be ready and what to bring before the scheduled day.
