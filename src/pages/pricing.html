A quote built from your trip

There’s no fixed price list. The cost depends on the distance, the pickup and destination, any mobility requirements, waiting time, and whether the trip is one-way, return or recurring.

What may affect your quote

Not every trip is priced the same way. These are the details that usually matter, and what we’ll ask about each.

FactorWhy it mattersWhat to tell us
Trip distance and route
Longer or more complex journeys take more time to plan and drive.
What to tell us: Pickup and destination addresses.
One-way or round trip
A return journey is planned, and quoted, as part of the same trip.
What to tell us: Whether you need a ride home.
Waiting time
If the driver waits during the appointment, that time may be included.
What to tell us: How long the appointment usually lasts.
Accessibility and vehicle needs
Some needs call for a particular vehicle or extra assistance, where available.
What to tell us: Wheelchair, walker, or help needed at the door.
Recurring arrangements
Regular trips may be quoted as a schedule rather than one ride at a time.
What to tell us: How often, and for how many weeks.
